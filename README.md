## About me

- I work at the intersection of **game design** and **interactive product thinking**
- I build **game prototypes**, explore **system-driven mechanics**, and design **mobile app concepts**
- I care about **clarity**, **usability**, **strong core ideas**, and how a product actually feels in use
- I’m especially interested in projects that combine **creativity**, **structure**, and **player/user experience**
